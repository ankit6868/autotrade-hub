## 4. THE SOLUTION / WHY CHOOSE US (core value props)

**Section title:** Why traders choose AutoTrade Hub

Four pillars (card grid):

### 🔬 1. Proof before you risk anything
Every strategy runs through a **leveraged futures backtester** on real KuCoin data — with liquidations, funding, fees and slippage modeled — and then a **walk-forward test** that splits history into windows. A real edge holds across windows; a curve-fit one only shines in one. If it doesn't pass, we tell you. No fake win rates.

### 🤖 2. A bot that learns to skip losers
Our **ML Loss-Filter** studies each strategy's own losing patterns and vetoes the entries most likely to fail — *without* changing your strategy's logic. It's meta-labeling (the López de Prado method), and it only ships if it beats the unfiltered strategy out-of-sample. Turn it on or off per bot, anytime.

### 🛡️ 3. Risk-first by design
Consecutive-loss **cooldown guardrails**, structural stop-losses, break-even trailing, partial take-profits (ARM), daily drawdown limits, liquidation-aware sizing, and per-session trading windows. The bot is built to *survive* a bad market, not just chase a good one.

### 🔑 4. You stay in control
Connect KuCoin with a **trade-only API key** (no withdrawal permission — ever). Funds never leave your exchange. Start in **paper mode** with a virtual balance, and only go live when *you* clear the safety gates.
